# Release-candidate limitations

1. **Production is not published or cleared for durable hosting.** SQLite persistence is verified across development restarts, not on ephemeral or multi-instance production storage. Do not deploy a persistence-dependent public app until storage and backup/restore are validated. Do not silently replace the database.
2. **No production account authentication.** Signed visitor cookies isolate fictional demonstration sessions; they are not customer accounts. Public paid-AI access needs an appropriate access policy.
3. **Reasoning allowance is exhausted:** five genuine OpenAI calls plus one genuine Anthropic call used the six-attempt cap. No paid retests or allowance increase without approval. Demo Mode remains credential-free. Actual invoices were not reconciled.
4. **Sarvam scope:** real API round trip verified in the development-only internal Voice Lab using generated input audio, not a human microphone. Main-chat voice is still browser-based. Physical-device capture, pronunciation/listening quality, Hindi/Kannada live speech and account credit balance are unverified. Four Sarvam calls had a conservative ₹1.27 pre-GST estimate; spending is disarmed.
5. **PWA scope:** production manifest, registered controlling worker, installation eligibility, offline shell, no API caching, waiting update and reconnection passed Chromium/Pixel-emulation checks. No physical Android installation or iPhone Safari installation is claimed. Development preview does not register the production worker.
6. **Offline is intentionally limited:** no cached private business responses, offline editing synchronization or queued submissions. Open inputs survive a connection loss in the current page, not an explicit reload or device shutdown. Updates warn before reloading.
7. **AI is probabilistic:** an early model response incorrectly treated total break-even as daily; grounding was corrected and subsequent checked results were correct. Financial panels, not prose, are authoritative. Contribution excludes rent, salaries and taxes.
8. **Recommendations:** discovery is curated and context-ranked; outcome-sensitive learning is deterministic with optional live-model interpretation, not autonomous business optimization. Sample results do not prove causality or real restaurant impact.
9. **Language:** four-language persistence/layout and deterministic workflows passed. Native-speaker linguistic review and four-language live-provider coverage were not performed. Saved conversations retain their original language.
10. **Market data:** real IndiaRestros listings are periodically updated source observations, not live offers, popularity, demand or sales evidence. Market-specific paid-model interpretation was not separately executed. A fresh REST request and cached repeat were executed and distinguished; unavailable data never becomes invented competitor facts.
11. **No external business execution:** WhatsApp, advertising, ordering and loyalty are manual experiment ideas, not executed messages, purchases, campaigns or transactions.
12. **Presentation:** original concepts and a licensed attributed breakfast photograph depict an illustrative product. Ravi and his business are fictional. Interaction recordings are genuine but silent.
13. **Build warnings:** approximately 1 MB minified application JS and two scaffold sourcemap-location notices are nonblocking. Performance optimization is deferred, not hidden.
14. **Distribution:** GitHub push is approved only for a private repository. Repository access/visibility must be verified before pushing; no forced overwrite, public visibility change or app/Pages publication is authorized.
