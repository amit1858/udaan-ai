# Three-minute human-led demo

## Before the demo
1. Open the working application preview at 390×844.
2. Settings → Reset demo. This resets only this browser's fictional records.
3. Check Settings: Demo Mode remains available; OpenAI should show configured/connected.
4. Three paid checks have been used at the time of initial evidence capture; maximum six total. Check the live verification report for subsequent usage before rehearsing paid calls.
5. Select Hinglish. Keep the credential-free recorded walkthrough ready.
6. Do not spend time debugging live AI on stage. Explicitly switch to Demo Mode if necessary and label it honestly.

## 0:00–0:20 — Ravi's problem
“Meet Ravi. Every morning he opens his restaurant, serves customers, manages staff and pays bills. He dreams of growing his business, but when does he get time to discover what is possible?”

## 0:20–0:40 — Introduce Udaan
“Udaan is an AI companion that understands your business, speaks your language, and grows with you.”
Show the image-led Home, not the business pulse.

## 0:40–1:15 — A new possibility
Discover → office breakfast subscriptions.
“He may not have considered serving the offices next door. Start with five opt-in pre-orders, not a new delivery operation.”
Label the story illustrative, not a real case study.

## 1:15–1:55 — Genuine AI
If a live call is available, choose OpenAI and ask:
“Mere restaurant mein weekdays par customers kam aa rahe hain. Kya karun?”
Show the model badge, contextual response, visual recommendation and follow-up.
Otherwise explicitly show the independent Demo Engine and use the saved live evidence in the review pack.

## 1:55–2:40 — Evidence changes the next idea
Open the ₹99 combo; show ₹99 price, ₹45 making cost, 100 target sales, ₹1,500 promotion.
“₹3,900 contribution after modeled costs, not net profit. Promotion breaks even at 28 units total.”
Create draft → approve → start. Load 70-unit sample → confirm → save.
Show ₹2,280 contribution and ₹600 visibility hypothesis.
Use a second separate experiment for 130 units: ₹5,520 and a different repeat-customer/expansion recommendation. Never overwrite a completed outcome.

## 2:40–2:50 — Language, same business
Switch to Kannada or Hindi; show unchanged experiment and localized learning.

## 2:50–3:00 — Close
“Most business software helps owners manage what their business is today. Udaan helps them imagine what their business could become tomorrow.”

## Rehearsal rules
- Synthetic outcomes do not prove real restaurant improvement.
- Browser recordings are actual interaction videos, without captured speech audio.
- Do not claim endorsements, traction, official judging scores or a guaranteed top-six place.
- Keep the final 25 minutes before the user's 15:00 IST deadline free of feature changes.
