### Modifications and share-alike

The inherited WebP assets are retained. Embedded photographic versions are converted to JPEG for SVG-renderer compatibility, then cropped, scaled and darkened within the launch layout. Photo adaptations remain **CC BY-SA 4.0**; no rights endorsement is implied. The photographic launch compositions (slides 1, 2, 4, 5 and cover, including those pages in the PDFs) are offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0** to preserve their share-alike obligations. Keep the photographer, source and license credit visible and link this credit file with source distributions.

These license notices apply to the identified photos and new photographic launch compositions. They do not relicense third-party software, existing application source, fonts, marks or separately retained original screenshots. The remaining newly authored launch layout/vector/copy work may also be reused under CC BY-SA 4.0. Existing product screenshots and Udaan brand assets remain subject to their original rights; this package does not grant unrelated application rights.

The recreated simple Udaan mark follows the existing navy/cyan/lime visual identity and is marketing artwork, not a counterfeit application capture. Actual screenshots are unaltered raster images except documented pixel crops. Their in-app food-image credits are retained in the original captures. No font binaries are distributed.
